# Frozen Paper Results Audit Pack

This directory was assembled on 2026-07-28 after the experiments were frozen; it contains
only evidence snapshots of completed experiments. No experiments were re-run, no harness
modified, no models called, and no metrics recomputed. The remote archives were attempted
read-only, but one host refused the connection and the other timed out.

Start from `EXPERIMENT_ANSWER_AUDIT.md`. It maps the existing frozen evidence to the paper's
experiment questions item by item, stating the conclusions that can be claimed, the
conclusions it cannot support, and the exact source path of each result.

## Contents

- `EXPERIMENT_ANSWER_AUDIT.md`: item-by-item evidence audit of the paper experiments.
- `cases/CASE_INDEX.md`: index of case purposes, experiment identities, and original sources.
- `cases/ccef_exploit_68_2/`: the final paper deliverable case, containing the complete
  evidence chain of the G2 high-score misjudgment, three official confirmations, the
  Failure Signature, the CCEF correction, and corroborating evidence of similar errors.

The copied cases are snapshots for reading convenience only. The original paths listed in
the audit remain the authoritative experiment records.
